Privacy Policy

LCS Tracker by AQAS
Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains how LCS Tracker by AQAS (“the app”) handles information when used by authorized courier/order-management staff and administrators.

Information we handle

  • Account and authentication information needed to sign in and control access.
  • Order and shipment information entered or scanned in the app, which may include customer names, phone numbers, delivery city/address where supplied, courier tracking/consignment numbers, shipment status, dates, amounts, weight, and staff assignment.
  • Receipt images or other photos that a user chooses to upload for order processing.
  • Technical and diagnostic information made available by the app's hosting, authentication, and infrastructure providers.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate authorized users, manage orders and receipts, retrieve or synchronize courier tracking details and statuses, display reports, prevent duplicate consignment numbers, maintain operational records, protect the service, and troubleshoot failures.

Service providers and sharing

The app uses Firebase services for authentication and cloud data storage and may send shipment identifiers or related information to the courier's API through a proxy service to retrieve tracking details. Those providers process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy practices. Access should be limited to authorized staff and administrators. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and security

Information is stored using the app's configured cloud services. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections where supported by the services. Access controls and account permissions are used to limit access; no internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention and deletion

Order, shipment, receipt, and audit information may be retained for business operations, dispute resolution, security, and applicable legal or accounting requirements. Authorized staff should contact the app administrator to request account deactivation or deletion and to ask about deletion of associated personal data. Some operational records may need to be retained or may be held in backups for a limited period.

Children

This business tool is not designed for children and is intended for authorized adult users.
