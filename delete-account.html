Last updated: 16 October 2026

How to request deletion

LCS Tracker accounts are provisioned by an administrator; users may not be able to delete their own account from within the app. To request account deactivation or deletion of personal information associated with an account, contact the app administrator at: [ADD A WORKING SUPPORT EMAIL BEFORE PUBLISHING].

Include your name, the account username, and a short description of the request. Do not send your password or verification codes. We may need to verify your identity and authorization before acting on a request.

What may be deleted

After review, we can deactivate an app account and assess deletion of personal information associated with it, where appropriate and technically possible.

Records that may be retained

Order, shipment, receipt, and audit records may be retained where needed for business operations, disputes, fraud prevention, security, or legal/accounting obligations. Backup copies may persist for a limited period before being overwritten. If records must be retained, the administrator can explain the reason and expected retention where applicable.

Processing

Requests are reviewed individually. We may contact you for clarification or verification. Do not include sensitive personal information beyond what is necessary to identify the account.
